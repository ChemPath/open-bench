# Research Ethics

ChemPath characterizes chemical structures. When a structure or preparation comes from traditional knowledge, Cloak & Quill Research follows these commitments:

- **Free, prior and informed consent (FPIC)** before any traditional-knowledge contribution is used, consistent with UNDRIP.
- **Community governance.** Partner communities keep decision rights, including the right to refuse, over how their knowledge is used.
- **Research partners, not data sources.** Contributions are attributed, and communities share in benefits under negotiated agreements.
- **Provenance.** The source of each traditional-knowledge record is recorded and kept attached to it.

This demo contains no traditional-knowledge records. Its reference library has only publicly catalogued structures, and its example formulations are synthetic.
