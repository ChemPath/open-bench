How to read this

Positions are computed chemistry, not biology. Nearby points have similar descriptor profiles. That says nothing about shared targets, efficacy or safety.

Drug-likeness rules are heuristics. Lipinski's rule of five (MW ≤ 500, logP ≤ 5, ≤ 5 H-bond donors, ≤ 10 acceptors; at most one violation) and Veber's rule (≤ 10 rotatable bonds, TPSA ≤ 140 Å²) describe typical orally absorbed drugs. Many useful natural products fall outside them.

Structural neighbours are ranked by Tanimoto similarity of Morgan fingerprints (radius 2, 2048 bits), a standard open method.

Data. 65 structures checked against published formulas and identifiers. Compounds marked "2D only" omit stereochemistry, which these 2D descriptors don't use. See the repository for the dataset and build script.

Open Bench is a set of public demonstrations built on open-source methods and public or synthetic data. Nothing on this page is a laboratory result or a prediction of biological activity.
